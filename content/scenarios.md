# Course scenarios

Fixed business contexts reused across every module's lessons and questions, so learners
build familiarity with a few systems instead of meeting a new one per item. Numbered;
topic frontmatter references them by number.

1. **Claims triage assistant** — insurance, high volume (40k/day), cost-sensitive, regulated.
   Classifies and routes claims; a wrong route delays a payout.
2. **Contract clause extractor** — legal, batch, precision-critical. Pulls named clause types
   out of long agreements for review; a missed clause reaches counsel, a false one wastes time.
3. **Support deflection agent** — multi-turn, latency-sensitive, measured on resolution and CSAT.
   Answers customer questions and escalates when it cannot.
4. **Policy knowledge assistant** — internal RAG over HR and compliance documents that are
   revised quarterly. Answers must be grounded and current.
5. **Research report agent** — long-horizon, open-ended, expensive. Gathers sources and drafts
   a briefing; success is coverage, groundedness, and source quality.
6. **Code migration agent in CI** — verifiable outcomes, gated by a test suite. Ports services
   to a new framework and opens pull requests.
